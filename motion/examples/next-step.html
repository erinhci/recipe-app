Egg Bites

Step 1 of 6

Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C). Generously grease 6 cups of a muffin tin with oil.

Thaw the frozen bell peppers in the microwave for 15 seconds. Press firmly between paper towels.

Coming up

  1. Thaw the frozen bell peppers in the microwave for 15 seconds.
  2. In a bowl, whisk 1 1/3 large eggs and 2 tsp water.
  3. Toss together 4 3/4 tsp diced chicken and 4 tsp broccoli.
  4. Pour the egg mixture over the fillings. Bake for 18–20 minutes.
